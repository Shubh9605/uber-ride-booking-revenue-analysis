# Uber Ride Booking & Revenue Performance Analysis

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing ride-booking operations and revenue performance through an interactive Power BI dashboard.

The dashboard provides insights into booking trends, vehicle category performance, revenue distribution, cancellation patterns, and customer and driver ratings.

## Business Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

- Analyze overall ride-booking performance.
- Understand booking status distribution.
- Compare performance across different vehicle categories.
- Analyze revenue by payment method.
- Identify customer and driver cancellation patterns.
- Evaluate customer and driver ratings.
- Identify business opportunities to improve operational efficiency.

## Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Data Visualization
- Business Intelligence

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Overall Analysis
Provides an overview of total bookings, booking status and booking trends.

### 2. Vehicle Type Analysis
Analyzes booking performance across different vehicle categories.

### 3. Revenue Analysis
Explores revenue, payment methods, ride distance and top customers.

### 4. Cancellation Analysis
Examines customer cancellations, driver cancellations and cancellation patterns.

### 5. Ratings Analysis
Provides an overview of customer and driver ratings.

## Key Business Questions

- How many bookings were received?
- What percentage of bookings were completed?
- What are the major reasons for ride cancellations?
- Which vehicle categories receive the most bookings?
- Which payment methods are commonly used?
- How is revenue distributed?
- How do customer and driver ratings compare?
